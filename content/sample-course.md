## Lesson 1: What is an AI agent?

### 1.1

**Question:** What makes an AI agent different from a regular chatbot?

- It can plan steps and use tools to finish a task on its own ✓
- It always gives longer answers
- It doesn't use a language model
- It only works offline

**Explanation:** A chatbot answers your message. An agent works toward a goal: it plans the steps, takes actions with tools (web search, email, spreadsheets) and checks the results.

### 1.2

**Question:** Which task is the best fit for an AI agent?

- "Find our 5 biggest competitors, check their pricing pages and put the results in a table" ✓
- "Write one sentence for a birthday card"
- "What is 2 + 2?"

**Explanation:** Agents shine at tasks with several steps that need tools and end with a clear result. A single short answer doesn't need an agent.

### 1.3

**Question:** An agent works in a loop. What is the right order?

- Plan → act → check the result → repeat ✓
- Act → stop
- Check → plan → stop
- Guess → finish

**Explanation:** The agent makes a plan, takes an action, looks at what happened and decides the next step, until the goal is reached.

### 1.4

**Question:** True or false: AI agents never make mistakes, so you don't need to check their work.

- True
- False ✓

**Explanation:** Agents can misread data or make up facts that sound right. Always review important work before you use it.

## Lesson 2: Agents in your marketing work

### 2.1

**Question:** You want an agent to draft a weekly social media report. What should you give it?

- A clear goal, access to the analytics data and an example of a good report ✓
- Just the words "make a report"
- The passwords to all company accounts
- Nothing, it will figure it out

**Explanation:** A clear goal, the right data and a good example lead to better results. Give the agent only the access it needs for the task.

### 2.2

**Question:** An agent wrote 10 ad variations. What is the best next step?

- Review them, pick the best ones and check the claims and brand tone before publishing ✓
- Publish all 10 right away
- Ask the agent if they are good and publish whatever it says
- Delete them and write your own

**Explanation:** Agents are great at producing drafts fast. A person stays in the loop to check facts, tone and quality before anything goes public.

### 2.3

**Question:** Which marketing task should an agent NOT do on its own?

- Approve the final budget for a new campaign ✓
- Collect competitor posts from last week
- Group customer reviews into themes
- Draft email subject lines

**Explanation:** Decisions about money and risk stay with people. The agent can prepare the information, but a person decides.

### 2.4

**Question:** An agent's report says: "Our Instagram followers grew by 300% last month." It sounds too good. What do you do?

- Check the number in the analytics tool yourself ✓
- Share it with the team right away
- Ask the agent for a nicer chart
- Ignore it

**Explanation:** Surprising numbers are a signal to double-check. The agent may have misread the data or made a mistake.
